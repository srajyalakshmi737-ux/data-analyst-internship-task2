
# Data Analyst Internship - Task 2

## Task
Data Visualization and Storytelling

## Objective
To create meaningful visualizations from the Superstore dataset
and identify useful business insights.

## Dataset
Superstore Orders Dataset

## Tool Used
Microsoft Power BI Desktop

## Visualizations Created

1. Total Sales
2. Total Profit
3. Total Quantity Sold
4. Sales by Category
5. Profit by Category
6. Sales by Region
7. Sales Trend Over Time
8. Sales by Segment

## Business Insights

- Sales performance varies across product categories.
- Technology, Furniture and Office Supplies contribute to overall sales.
- Profit differs across product categories.
- Sales performance varies across regions.
- Sales change over time.
- Customer segments contribute differently to overall sales.

## Deliverables

- Power BI Dashboard
- Power BI Report (.pbix)
- Dashboard Screenshot
